Donation Performance by Payment Method

This chart illustrates total monetary contributions aggregated across specific payment methods (e.g., Wire Transfer, Check, Stripe, PayPal). Stakeholders can use this category breakdown to evaluate payment gateway volume and identify liquidity trends across different donor types.

Resource Aid Volume by Distribution Hub

This bar chart displays the volume of physical aid supplies distributed across regional facilities, broken down into medical items and food/water rations. Executive leadership can review these totals to identify supply chain bottlenecks and rebalance inventory across regional hubs.

Campaign Revenue vs. Marketing Budget

This visualization compares gross revenue generated against marketing expenditure for each major fundraising campaign in `dim_campaigns`. Stakeholders can evaluate these side-by-side figures to assess acquisition efficiency and guide upcoming marketing budget allocations.
